Steps to Perform
1. Open Google Chrome browser.
2. Visit the official website: MetaMask Official Website.
3. Click on Get MetaMask.
4. Select Google Chrome.
5. Click on Add to Chrome.
6. Click on Add Extension.
7. Open the MetaMask extension.
8. Click on Create a New Wallet.
9. Accept the terms and conditions.
10. Create a strong password.
11. Click on Create Wallet.
12. MetaMask will show a Secret Recovery Phrase.
13. Write down the recovery phrase and keep it safe.
14. Confirm the recovery phrase.
15. Complete the wallet setup.
16. Your MetaMask wallet is successfully created.
17. Copy your wallet address to receive cryptocurrency.
18. Click on Send to send cryptocurrency to another wallet.
19. Enter the receiver's wallet address.
20. Enter the amount and confirm the transaction.

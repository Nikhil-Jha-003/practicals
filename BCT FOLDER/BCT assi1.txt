A. Installation of MetaMask
1. Open Google Chrome browser.
2. Visit the official website: MetaMask Download.
3. Click on Get MetaMask.
4. Select Google Chrome.
5. Click on Add to Chrome.
6. Click on Add Extension.
7. Open the MetaMask extension.
8. Click on Create a New Wallet.
9. Set a strong password.
10. Securely save your Secret Recovery Phrase.
11. Complete the verification process.
12. Your MetaMask wallet is ready.

B. Connect to Sepolia Test Network
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Click on the network selector.
3. Enable Show Test Networks.
4. Select Sepolia Test Network.
5. Copy your wallet address.
6. Open the MetaMask Faucet or Sepolia Faucet.
7. Request test ETH using your wallet address.
8. Wait until test ETH is received.

C. Send Ether and Study Transaction Fees
1. Open MetaMask.
2. Select the Sepolia network.
3. Click on Send.
4. Enter another Sepolia wallet address.
5. Enter the amount of ETH (e.g. 0.001 ETH).
6. Click on Next.
7. Check the estimated gas fee.
8. Observe the total transaction amount.
9. Click on Confirm.
10. Wait for the transaction to complete.
11. Open the transaction details.
12. Note the transaction hash and gas fee.
13. Open Sepolia Etherscan.
14. Paste your transaction hash in the search box.
15. Observe the transaction details and Ether spent.


D. Study Ether Spending per Transaction
1. Open the transaction details in MetaMask.
2. Observe the following details:
   - Transaction amount
   - Gas limit
   - Gas price
   - Network fee
   - Total amount spent
3. Open the transaction on Sepolia Etherscan.
4. Check the actual transaction fee.
5. Record the details for different transactions.
